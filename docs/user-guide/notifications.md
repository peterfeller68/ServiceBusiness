# Notifications

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Notifications are sent or logged for important workflow events such as account approval decisions, completed visits, and invoices.

## Notification Preferences

Open Profile to update:

- Notification email
- Email notifications

If email notifications are disabled, the application records the notification as suppressed and does not send it.

## Test Users

Test users are handled safely. In DevTest mode or when a recipient is a test user, email jobs can mark messages as sent without sending through the provider. A configured test recipient can also receive rerouted test messages.

## Email Log

Use Email Log under Logs to review message status, recipients, subject, failure reason, send date, and body details when your role has access.
