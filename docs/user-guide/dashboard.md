# Dashboard

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

After signing in, the application opens `/dashboard`. The dashboard changes based on your role and access status.

## Pending Approval

If your company access is waiting for approval, the dashboard shows Pending Access. Features unlock after a business owner or system administrator approves your request.

## System Administrator Dashboard

System Administrators see:

- System Health metrics for service clients, business clients, users, materials, services, and service packages.
- Pool Equipment and Pool Configurations metrics in Pool mode.
- Approvals with individual approval toggles and Approve All.
- System Workspaces links for focused admin pages.

The System Administrator dashboard filters service clients and users to the current app mode.

## Business Owner Dashboard

Business Owners see:

- System Health metrics for business clients, users, materials, services, and service packages.
- Pool Configurations in Pool mode.
- Approvals with individual approval toggles and Approve All.
- Assigned Visits Today.
- Upcoming Visits.
- Unscheduled and Scheduled Visits.
- System Workspaces links for business-client, user, material, service, and service-package management.

Assigned Visits Today lets business owners complete, edit, or inspect visits assigned to them for today.

Upcoming Visits is view-only and lists future visits assigned to the signed-in owner.

Unscheduled and Scheduled Visits lists visits that still need assignment or schedule completion. Business owners can assign those visits to an active business owner or employee.

## Business Employee Dashboard

Business Employees see:

- Assigned Visits Today.
- Upcoming Visits.
- Recently Completed Visits.

Today's visits can be marked complete. Recently completed visits can be moved back to In Progress.

When editing an assigned visit, employees can update customer-facing notes, service-provider notes, internal notes, completed planned services, and out-of-scope services.

## Business Client Dashboard

Business Clients see:

- Upcoming Visits.
- Service Package.
- Pool Configuration in Pool mode.
- Completed Visits.

Upcoming Visits is expanded by default. Business Clients can edit only Notes To Service Provider.

The Service Package panel shows the package assigned to the business client. If the business client does not have one assigned directly, the dashboard uses the service client's package when available.

Completed Visits is the service history view for the client.

## Independent Home Owner Dashboard

Independent Home Owners see:

- Pool Equipment.
- Service History.

Pool Equipment lists configured equipment with category, manufacturer, equipment, and comment. The info action shows additional equipment details.

Service History lets the homeowner add, edit, and delete their own service history entries.

## Pool and Landscape Modes

Dashboard branding and hero imagery follow the current app mode. Pool-only dashboard items, such as Pool Equipment and Pool Configuration, are hidden in Landscape mode.
