# Account Approval and User Management

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Use user management to review registered users, approve access requests, and control who can work inside a company.

## System Administrator Users

System Administrators use System Administrator / Users.

The page shows:

- User counts
- Active users
- System administrators
- Pending access
- Disabled users

System Administrators can edit user contact fields, notification email, test-user flag, and email-notification preference. They can also enable or disable users and grant or remove System Administrator access.

Self-protection rules prevent a System Administrator from disabling their own account. The system also keeps at least one active System Administrator.

## Business Owner Users

Business Owners use Settings / Company Users.

The page shows:

- Company name
- Active users
- Pending access requests

Business Owners can approve or reject pending Business Employee and Business Client access. Approved users can be activated, deactivated, or reassigned to a different company role.

Business Owners cannot deactivate their own Business Owner access or remove their own Business Owner role. The system keeps at least one active Business Owner for the company.

## Pending Users

Pending Business Employees and Business Clients can sign in, but feature navigation stays limited until their request is approved.
