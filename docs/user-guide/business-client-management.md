# Business Client Management

Last reviewed: 2026-08-05

Business Clients are the customers or properties served by a service client business.

## Opening Business Clients

- System Administrators open System Administrator / Business Clients.
- Business Owners open Settings / Business Clients.

System Administrators can see business clients across service clients matching the current Pool or Landscape mode. Business Owners see only their own service client's business clients.

## What You Can See

The Business Clients table shows:

- Status
- Company for System Administrators
- Name
- Client Type
- Service Package
- Email
- Phone

## Creating Or Editing A Business Client

Use Create or the edit action to open the inline editor.

The editor includes business client type, service package, name, primary contact, email, phone, service address, rate override, access notes, and active status. System Administrators also choose the service client company when creating a business client.

## Archive And Reactivate

The active toggle controls whether the business client is active. The delete action currently archives the business client by setting it inactive.

Inactive business clients are not available for new business-client registration requests.
