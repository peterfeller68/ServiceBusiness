# Getting Started

Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

Use Help to find current application guidance while you work.

## Sign In

- Public users can open Home and Help before signing in.
- Registered users sign in with Google when Google authentication is configured.
- Test users can use the test sign-in flow when DevTest mode is enabled.
- New users can register as Business Owner, Business Employee, Business Client, or Home Owner.
- Business Employees and Business Clients wait for business owner approval before full access is available.
- Independent Home Owners do not require business approval.

## After Sign In

- Dashboard is the main workspace after sign-in.
- Navigation changes based on your role and active company access.
- Pending users can continue to use Help while waiting for approval.

## User Guide

Open Help / User Guide to browse feature-specific guides such as Registration and Authentication, Dashboard, Visit Scheduling, Invoices, Jobs, and Logs.
