# Registration and Authentication

Last reviewed: 2026-08-06

Use registration to create the right kind of account, then sign in with Google or, in DevTest mode, a seeded test account.

## Sign In

- Select Sign In from the landing page.
- Use Sign in with Google for real accounts.
- In DevTest mode, seeded test users can enter a test user id or email and select Skip Google Auth.
- After sign-in, the app opens Dashboard.

If your account is disabled, sign-in is blocked.

## Register

Select Register from the landing page, then choose one account type.

## Business Owner

Business owners create a new service client business.

1. Choose Business owner.
2. Continue with Google, or skip Google only in DevTest mode.
3. Enter your account details and business profile.
4. Complete registration.

The account is active immediately as a Business Owner, and the app opens Dashboard.

## Business Employee

Business employees request access to an existing business.

1. Choose Business employee.
2. Continue with Google, or skip Google only in DevTest mode.
3. Choose the business you want to join.
4. Complete registration.

The account stays pending until a Business Owner approves access.

## Business Client

Business clients request access to a specific client address.

1. Choose Business client.
2. Continue with Google, or skip Google only in DevTest mode.
3. Choose the business.
4. Choose the business client address.
5. Complete registration.

The account stays pending until a Business Owner approves access.

## Home Owner

Independent home owners manage their own home records without joining a business.

1. Choose Home owner.
2. Continue with Google, or skip Google only in DevTest mode.
3. Enter your account details, home address, and optional access notes.
4. Choose a subscription plan.
5. Complete registration.

No business approval is required. The account opens Dashboard, starts the configured subscription trial, and owner-scoped pool equipment records are created automatically in Pool mode.

## Pending Approval

Pending Business Employee and Business Client users can sign in and see pending approval status. More navigation options appear after a Business Owner approves access.
